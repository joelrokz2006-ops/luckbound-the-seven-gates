HOW TO PLAY

1. The 7-Round Journey

In each round, 3 gates appear: Ember (Flame), Tide (Water), and Gale (Wind). Click or press keys 1, 2, or 3 to choose a gate.

2. Transparent Fate Probabilities

✦ Starfall (45%): Awards base score (100 pts x Multiplier).
✦ Lucky Echo (25%): Awards 50 pts x Multiplier and adds +1 Streak.
✦ Guardian Sigil (15%): Gives 1 shield that blocks the next Void.
✦ Void (15%): Shatters a shield if held, or resets unbanked score to 0 and ends run.

3. Rising Stakes & Multipliers

Gates 1–2: 1.0x | Gates 3–4: 1.25x | Gates 5–6: 1.5x | Gate 7: 2.0x Final Gate Multiplier!

4. Bank vs Continue

After each survival, Bank your run score safely to personal best or Continue to risk for higher rewards!
