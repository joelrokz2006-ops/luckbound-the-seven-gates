## Audio Assets & Synthesizer

All sound effects (gate hover, gate select, Starfall chime, Lucky Echo pulse, Guardian Sigil shield, Void collapse, Shield block clang, Bank harp flourish, and button clicks) and the ambient celestial soundtrack are 100% original procedural waveforms generated mathematically via `scripts/procedural_audio.gd`.

All visual assets, including the celestial background, floating island silhouettes, dynamic starfields, elemental gate arches (Ember, Tide, Gale), and particle animations are generated dynamically via Godot's vector CanvasItem drawing routines.
